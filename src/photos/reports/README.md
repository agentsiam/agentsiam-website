# reports

One page of a real feasibility report, with the numbers blurred or redacted.

The product is "a written analysis with real numbers, ending in a Go or a No-Go". Every
competitor claims something similar and none of them show you one. A single photographed or
exported page — enough to see it is a real document with real structure, not enough to give
away a client's figures — makes the claim checkable.

**Before adding anything here:** the report belongs to the client it was written for. Get
their permission, and redact the property, the address and the figures. If that leaves too
little to be worth showing, a page from a sample or template report is the honest fallback,
captioned as such.
