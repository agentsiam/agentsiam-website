# team

Portraits of the people who run AgentSiam. One file each.

The owner page claims "we live here", "we visit the properties", "someone local reads the
message at 11pm". Right now that is a claim with no face attached, and it is the single
weakest thing about `/how-it-works`.

**What works:** each founder photographed at a property or on a Chiang Mai street, in
daylight, looking at the camera. Not a studio backdrop, not a headshot against a white wall,
not a stock-looking "team in a meeting" shot — the whole argument is that these are real
people in a real city.

**Description to type into each file:** the person's name and where it was taken, e.g.
"Paul at Lotus House in Chang Khlan". That becomes the alt text.

The moment a photo lands here, the "For owners" panel on the homepage picks it up and both
fork panels switch from flat colour to photographs together.
