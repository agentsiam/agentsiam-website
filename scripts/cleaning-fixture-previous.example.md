# Cleaning and laundry schedule, Lotus House

An invented baseline for the cleaning app's fixture, in the shape `save_note.py` writes. It is
never a record of anything that was posted and it belongs to no run: it exists so that
`build_note.py --previous` has something to diff the example payload against, and the fixture
therefore carries change lines in its remarks block. Without it the app's one scheduled-changes
screen renders empty and nothing exercises it.
