{{ T.gatesTitle }}
Otherwise it is a sales document with a fee attached. Two things have to be true before we will manage a property, and they are different questions:
Gate one · your side
At cautious occupancy, short-let has to beat renting it long-term.
Not at the optimistic case — at the conservative one. We pull a real local long-term comparable at the time of the study rather than assuming one, because that number is what decides the verdict.
Gate two · our side
The property has to be worth managing properly.
Below a certain size the fee does not fund the attention the property needs, and doing it badly helps nobody. This gate applies to management only — anyone can buy the study.
If your case works but we are not the right size of manager for it, we will say that too, and point you somewhere better.
