AgentSiam — booking portal wireframe · v2
One homepage, two personas, guest search leading
Single source of truth for the AgentSiam site. Home carries both audiences: guests lead on position (search sits in the hero), owners get equal weight further down (fork panel plus a full staircase section). Built for target state — hundreds of Chiang Mai units — so nothing here is tagged deferred. Structure and hierarchy only, not final copy or visual polish.
Architecture — decided
source of truth Beds24 Channel manager. Owns availability, rates, bookings, payment.
→ push
new layer Middleware Receives Beds24 webhooks, normalises into the property schema, serves the frontend. Periodic full reconcile catches missed events.
→ read
what we design React frontend Search, filters, map, property pages, trip summary. Never calls Beds24 directly on page load.
→ handoff
money moves here Beds24 payment iframe Re-validates availability and price at the moment of payment. Final authority.
Consequence to design for: our availability and prices are a cache, so they can be briefly stale. That is acceptable only because Beds24 re-validates at handoff — which means the flow needs an honest failure state for "these dates looked free, Beds24 disagrees at checkout." It is wireframed at Level 3. Two things this buys: rates and availability become crawlable page text rather than iframe content, and the brief's [001]–[026] field IDs become the middleware's normalised model instead of hand-entered fields.

EACH STEP — what you get, then what you do not Every step card pairs its inclusions with an exclusions callout in a contrasting box. Not a footnote — the same visual weight as the sell. 1 Feasibility — site visit, written analysis, one-hour call, Go/No-Go. Limit: a projection is not a promise; judged on the conservative case.
2 Permission — Hotel Act assessment, safety review, documents, filing under power of attorney. Limit: government fees and fire remediation excluded, and the government's decision cannot be guaranteed.
3 Management — listings, direct booking site, Beds24, guest comms, reviews, compliance. Limit: supplier costs sit with the owner.
